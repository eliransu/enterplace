AWS IAM Identity Center signs people in to the admin console as a customer managed SAML 2.0 application (`console.saml`). It sends the IDs of the person's groups, and Enterplace maps those IDs to a console role and, at `enterplace login`, to a team.

The examples use `https://enterplace.example.com` for your Enterplace address.

<Aside type="note">
These steps follow AWS's public documentation as read on 2026-11-15. They were not run against IAM Identity Center. The points to test first are marked below.
</Aside>

## OpenID Connect

IAM Identity Center does not offer OpenID Connect sign-in to an application of your own. Its customer managed applications are SAML 2.0, or OAuth 2.0 for trusted identity propagation to AWS services, which issues no ID token to a web application. Use SAML, or put an OpenID Connect provider in front (the identity source behind Identity Center, such as [Okta](/governance/identity-providers/okta/) or [Microsoft Entra ID](/governance/identity-providers/entra-id/), connects to Enterplace directly).

## SAML 2.0

A SAML application needs an organization instance of IAM Identity Center.

<Steps>

1. In the IAM Identity Center console, choose **Applications**, open the **Customer managed** tab and choose **Add application**.

2. Under **Setup preference**, choose **I have an application I want to set up**. Under **Application type**, choose **SAML 2.0**. Choose **Next**.

3. Enter a **Display name**. Under **IAM Identity Center metadata**, download the **IAM Identity Center SAML metadata file** and the **IAM Identity Center certificate**.

4. Under **Application properties**, set **Application start URL** to `https://enterplace.example.com/console/saml/start`. A click on the access portal tile then opens Enterplace, which starts the sign-in itself. Enterplace refuses a sign-in started at Identity Center.

5. Under **Application metadata**, choose **Manually type your metadata values**:
   - **Application ACS URL**: `https://enterplace.example.com/console/saml/acs`
   - **Application SAML audience**: `https://enterplace.example.com/console/saml/metadata`

   Choose **Submit**.

6. On the application's page, choose **Actions** > **Edit attribute mapping** and add:

   | Attribute in the application | Value in IAM Identity Center | Format |
   |---|---|---|
   | `Subject` | `${user:email}` | `emailAddress` |
   | `email` | `${user:email}` | `unspecified` |
   | `groups` | `${user:groups}` | `unspecified` |

   Choose **Save changes**.

7. Under **Assigned users**, choose **Assign users** and assign the groups that should reach the console. Nested groups are not supported: assign the group a person is a direct member of.

8. Find each group's ID: choose **Groups**, open the group, and copy its group ID. {/* field label not confirmed in AWS's public docs, 2026-11 */}

</Steps>

```yaml title="enterplace.policy.yaml"
console:
  saml:
    idp_entity_id: https://portal.sso.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/saml/assertion/MDEyMzQ1Njc4OTAxX2lucy0x
    idp_sso_url: https://portal.sso.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/saml/assertion/MDEyMzQ1Njc4OTAxX2lucy0x
    idp_certificate: |
      -----BEGIN CERTIFICATE-----
      MIIDBzCCAe+gAwIBAgIFAJ...
      -----END CERTIFICATE-----
    sp_entity_id: https://enterplace.example.com/console/saml/metadata
    acs_url: https://enterplace.example.com/console/saml/acs
    email_attribute: email
    role_attribute: groups
    admin_values: ["a1b2c3d4-5678-90ab-cdef-EXAMPLE11111"]
    read_only_values: ["a1b2c3d4-5678-90ab-cdef-EXAMPLE22222"]
    allowed_domains: [example.com]
```

Copy `idp_entity_id` (the `entityID`) and `idp_sso_url` (the `SingleSignOnService` location for the HTTP-Redirect binding) from the metadata file you downloaded. The two URLs above are placeholders and are not the form AWS documents.

- **Groups arrive as IDs.** `${user:groups}` sends each group's ID, not its name (AWS's user guide does not list this value; its OpenSearch and Client VPN guides use it), so `admin_values`, `read_only_values` and every `sso_values` list hold group IDs. Comment each with the group's name.
- **Certificates.** Identity Center makes one certificate per application, valid five years. To rotate, generate a new one under **Manage certificate**, put both PEM blocks in `idp_certificate`, set the new one active, then remove the old block.

<Aside type="caution">
Test three things before you roll this out. Enterplace sends its request with the HTTP-Redirect binding, and AWS's documentation describes only a request sent by POST. Decode one response to see what `groups` holds. And if the downloaded certificate was made with SHA-1, generate one with SHA-256 under **Manage certificate**: Enterplace refuses SHA-1 signatures with `saml_signature`.
</Aside>

## Teams and custom roles

The values work beyond the two built-in roles. A custom role under `roles:` takes them in `sso_values`, and so does a team under `teams:`. At `enterplace login`, a person listed in a team's `members` gets that team, otherwise the first team whose `sso_values` match one of their values, otherwise `self_issue.team`.

## Check it

<Steps>

1. Open `https://enterplace.example.com/console` in a private window and click the sign-in button.

2. Sign in at the AWS access portal as a member of the admin group. The console opens with the admin role.

3. Sign in as a member of the read-only group only. The console opens read only.

</Steps>

A refusal with `sso_role` means no group ID matched. Check the IDs in the policy against **Groups** in Identity Center, and that the person is a direct member. A refusal with `saml_replay` means the sign-in started at the access portal: set the **Application start URL**.
