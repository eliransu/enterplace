PingOne can sign people in to the admin console with an OIDC Web App (`console.sso`) or a SAML application (`console.saml`). In both, an attribute mapping sends the names of the person's groups, and Enterplace maps them to a console role and, at `enterplace login`, to a team.

The examples use `https://enterplace.example.com` for your Enterplace address, the environment ID `0a1b2c3d-4e5f-6789-abcd-ef0123456789`, and the groups `enterplace-admins` and `enterplace-viewers`.

<Aside type="note">
These steps follow PingOne's public documentation as read on 2026-11-15. They were not run against a PingOne environment. PingFederate is not covered: connect it as any OpenID Connect or SAML 2.0 provider, with the values on the [Single sign-on](/governance/single-sign-on/) and [SAML 2.0](/governance/saml/) pages.
</Aside>

## OpenID Connect

<Steps>

1. In the PingOne admin console, go to **Applications** > **Applications** and click the plus icon. Enter a name, select **OIDC Web App** as the application type, and click **Save**.

2. On the **Configuration** tab, click the pencil icon and set:
   - **Response Type**: **Code**
   - **Grant Type**: **Authorization Code**
   - **PKCE Enforcement**: the S256 option. Enterplace always sends an S256 challenge.
   - **Redirect URIs**: `https://enterplace.example.com/console/sso/callback`
   - **Token Endpoint Authentication Method**: **Client Secret Basic**

   {/* the PKCE Enforcement option labels, and S256 enforcement together with a client secret, are not confirmed in PingOne's public docs, 2026-11 */}

3. On the **Resources** tab, add the `email` and `profile` scopes to `openid`.

4. On the **Attribute Mappings** tab, click the pencil icon, then **Add**. Enter `groups` under **Attributes** and choose **Group Names** under **PingOne Mappings**. Add `email` mapped to **Email Address** the same way, and save. Mapped attributes go in the ID token whatever scopes the request asks for.

5. On the **Access** tab, add the two groups under the group membership policy, so only their members reach the application.

6. Copy the **Client ID**, the **Client Secret** and the **Environment ID** from the **Configuration** tab, and turn the application on with the toggle at the top.

</Steps>

```yaml title="enterplace.policy.yaml"
console:
  sso:
    issuer: https://auth.pingone.com/0a1b2c3d-4e5f-6789-abcd-ef0123456789/as
    client_id: 6f1e2d3c-4b5a-6978-8a9b-0c1d2e3f4a5b
    client_secret_env: ENTERPLACE_SSO_SECRET
    redirect_url: https://enterplace.example.com/console/sso/callback
    allowed_domains: [example.com]
    role_claim: groups
    admin_values: [enterplace-admins]
    read_only_values: [enterplace-viewers]
```

The issuer is `https://auth.pingone.<region domain>/<environment ID>/as`, or `https://<custom domain>/as` with a custom domain. Read the exact value from `issuer` in the discovery document at `<issuer>/.well-known/openid-configuration`. Set the secret where Enterplace runs: `ENTERPLACE_SSO_SECRET=...`.

<Aside type="caution">
Decode one ID token before you rely on this setup. Enterplace needs `email` in the ID token, and refuses a token that says `email_verified: false` with `sso_email`. PingOne's documentation does not say which of the two it puts in the ID token for the `email` scope, which is why step 4 maps `email` by hand.
</Aside>

## SAML 2.0

<Steps>

1. Go to **Applications** > **Applications**, click the plus icon, enter a name and select the SAML application type. Click **Configure**.

2. Choose **Import from URL** and give `https://enterplace.example.com/console/saml/metadata`. Or choose **Manually Enter**:
   - **ACS URLs**: `https://enterplace.example.com/console/saml/acs`
   - **Entity ID**: `https://enterplace.example.com/console/saml/metadata`

3. On the **Configuration** tab, check:
   - **Sign Assertion** selected (or **Sign Assertion & Response**)
   - **Signing Algorithm**: `RSA_SHA256`
   - **Encryption** off: Enterplace does not read encrypted assertions
   - **Subject NameID format**: `urn:oasis:names:tc:SAML:1.1:nameid-format:emailAddress`
   - **Enforce Signed Authentication Request** off: Enterplace does not sign its requests

4. On the **Attribute Mappings** tab, map the subject (`saml_subject`) to **Email Address** {/* the saml_subject label is not confirmed in PingOne's public docs, 2026-11 */}, and add `email` mapped to **Email Address** and `groups` mapped to **Group Names**.

5. On the **Access** tab, add the two groups, and turn **Application Portal Display** off. A click on the portal tile starts the sign-in at PingOne, which Enterplace refuses. People start at `/console`.

6. On the **Configuration** tab, click **Download Signing Certificate** and choose the X509 PEM format. Copy the issuer ID and the single sign-on URL from the application's connection details, or read all three from its metadata. Turn the application on.

   {/* the console labels for the issuer ID, the SSO URL and the metadata link are not confirmed in PingOne's public docs, 2026-11 */}

</Steps>

{/* not checked: the certificate is a placeholder */}
```yaml title="enterplace.policy.yaml"
console:
  saml:
    idp_entity_id: https://auth.pingone.com/0a1b2c3d-4e5f-6789-abcd-ef0123456789
    idp_sso_url: https://auth.pingone.com/0a1b2c3d-4e5f-6789-abcd-ef0123456789/saml20/idp/sso
    idp_certificate: |
      -----BEGIN CERTIFICATE-----
      MIIDejCCAmKgAwIBAgIGAY...
      -----END CERTIFICATE-----
    sp_entity_id: https://enterplace.example.com/console/saml/metadata
    acs_url: https://enterplace.example.com/console/saml/acs
    email_attribute: email
    role_attribute: groups
    admin_values: [enterplace-admins]
    read_only_values: [enterplace-viewers]
    allowed_domains: [example.com]
```

The entity ID is `https://auth.pingone.<region domain>/<environment ID>`. Take `idp_sso_url` from the application's metadata (the `SingleSignOnService` location for the HTTP-Redirect binding) rather than from this example.

## Teams and custom roles

The values work beyond the two built-in roles. A custom role under `roles:` takes them in `sso_values`, and so does a team under `teams:`. At `enterplace login`, a person listed in a team's `members` gets that team, otherwise the first team whose `sso_values` match one of their values, otherwise `self_issue.team`.

## Check it

<Steps>

1. Open `https://enterplace.example.com/console` in a private window and click the sign-in button.

2. Sign in at PingOne as a member of `enterplace-admins`. The console opens with the admin role.

3. Sign in as a member of `enterplace-viewers` only. The console opens read only.

</Steps>

A refusal with `sso_role` means the groups held no configured value. Check the `groups` mapping is **Group Names**, not **Group IDs**, and the exact group names in the policy.
