Every key names a team, and rules, budgets and limits are written per team. The policy's `teams:` adds what a key cannot say: who owns a team, who is in it, and which identity provider groups put a person in it. `gantry login` uses it to put each developer's key in the right team, and console sign-in records it.

## Example

```yaml title="gantry.policy.yaml"
version: 1
providers:
  - name: openai
    kind: openai
    url: https://api.openai.com
    key_env: OPENAI_API_KEY
    models: [gpt-4o-mini]
rules:
  - name: payments
    principals: { team: payments }
    models: [gpt-4o-mini]
    budget: { usd: 500, per: month }
teams:
  - name: payments
    owner: maya@example.com
    members: [omer@example.com]
    sso_values: [payments-eng]
    metadata: { cost_center: "4410" }
roles:
  - name: payments-lead
    permissions: [see_keys, issue_keys, revoke_keys, see_spend, see_records]
    teams: [payments]
    sso_values: [payments-leads]
```

## Fields

| Field | Type | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `name` | string | required | The team keys and rules name. |
| `owner` | string | none | Email of the person answerable for the team. |
| `members` | list | `[]` | Emails of the people in the team. A person is in one team only. |
| `sso_values` | list | `[]` | Values of the identity provider's role claim (`console.sso.role_claim`) or SAML role attribute that put a person in this team. |
| `metadata` | map | `{}` | Your own notes, such as a cost center. At most 32 entries. |

Teams that only keys name keep working: `teams:` is needed only for owners, members and groups.

## Which team a person is in

At `gantry login` and at console sign-in, a person's team is:

1. the team whose `members` lists their email, else
2. the first team whose `sso_values` holds one of their group values, else
3. for `gantry login`, `console.sso.self_issue.team` when it is written.

When `teams:` places people (some team has `members` or `sso_values`) and places this person nowhere, and `self_issue.team` is not written, `gantry login` refuses with `sso_team` and says to add them. With no such teams, `gantry login` puts keys in `self_issue.team` (`developers` unless set), as before.

## Roles kept to teams

A [console role](/governance/policy/#console-roles) with `teams:` keeps its holders to those teams, as `org:` keeps them to an organization; `customers:` keeps them to those end customers. In the console and the admin API such a person:

- sees only the keys of their teams, and only the audit records, requests, analytics and spend of their teams;
- issues keys only in their teams (and, kept to one customer, for that customer), and cannot move a key out;
- revokes, rotates and changes only their teams' keys;
- may give a new key only a role kept within the same teams;
- cannot set kill switches, change provider credentials, edit the policy or change teams, which act on the whole install.

Each refusal is recorded with `not_permitted` and names what the role is kept to.

## The teams API

`GET /admin/teams` lists every team, from `teams:` and from keys, with its owner, members, groups, metadata, whether the policy lists it (`in_policy`) and its keys. `GET /admin/teams/<name>` shows one. Reading needs `see_keys`; a role kept to teams sees its teams.

```bash
curl -s https://gantry.example.com/admin/teams -H "Authorization: Bearer $GANTRY_ADMIN_KEY"
```

Changing a team saves a new policy version, so it needs [the policy kept in the store](/operations/store/) (`store.policy_from: store`) and `edit_policy` in a role kept to no part of the install. Each change is checked as `gantry check` checks a file, recorded in the audit chain, waits for a second admin when `store.approval: true`, and is live on every gateway within 2 seconds.

```bash
curl -s https://gantry.example.com/admin/teams -X POST \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $GANTRY_ADMIN_KEY" -H "X-Gantry-Note: new team" \
  -d '{"name": "web", "owner": "omer@example.com", "sso_values": ["web-eng"]}'
curl -s https://gantry.example.com/admin/teams/web -X PUT \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $GANTRY_ADMIN_KEY" -d '{"owner": "omer@example.com", "members": ["dana@example.com"]}'
curl -s https://gantry.example.com/admin/teams/web -X DELETE -H "Authorization: Bearer $GANTRY_ADMIN_KEY"
```

`PUT` replaces the team's fields with the body. The answer is the new version, as `PUT /admin/policy` answers ([Console editing](/operations/console-editing/)). A policy kept in the file is refused with `policy_in_file`: change `teams:` in the file.

<Aside type="note">
A form edit writes the policy without its comments, as the console's Models page does. Change teams in the policy editor to keep them.
</Aside>
