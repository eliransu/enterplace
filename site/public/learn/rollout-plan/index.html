The rollout on one page, to forward.

The steps come in order, and you set the pace: stay on a step for a day or a quarter. Each step works without the next.

StepWhat happensWho does itWhat changes for your peopleYou can then answer
1. SeeGantry runs as one container. AI tools point at it. Observe mode: nothing is blocked.A platform engineerOne settings change in each toolWhich tools and models do we use, and what do they cost?
2. KnowEach person gets their own key, or signs in with the company account.The same engineer, or your identity teamThey swap a shared key for their ownWho uses what, by person and team?
3. LimitA monthly budget per team, recorded first and then enforced. A stop button for a person, team or model.You pick the amount. The engineer adds one lineNothing until a team reaches its budget. Then requests are refused until the next month or a raised budgetWhat is the most we can spend this month? Can we stop it today?
4. ProtectA check for secrets in prompts, on trial until a date you pick, then blocking.Security picks the date. The engineer adds the linesAfter the date, a prompt that holds a key or password is refused with a message that says whyWhat stops a password from being sent to a model?
5. ProveThe audit log is kept on durable storage and its chain is verified.The engineerNothingCan we show an auditor every request, and that the record is complete?

What you decide

Before step 1
Who runs Gantry and where. One container and your existing provider keys.
At step 3
The monthly amount per team, and the day you turn enforcement on. Until that day Gantry records what it would refuse and refuses nothing.
At step 4
The date the secrets check starts blocking.
At step 5
Whether you need Gantry Enterprise for SIEM export, retention and legal hold.

What stays the same

Your provider accounts
And your contracts with them. Gantry holds the provider keys, and your people stop holding them.
Prompts and answers
Gantry forwards them unchanged unless a guardrail you added masks or blocks something.
The tools
People keep the tools they chose.

Three example rollouts

These are scenarios written to show the steps, with real policy lines. They are not customers. Gantry is pre-1.0 and has no customer stories to publish yet.

Example: a 200-person fintech rolling out Claude Code

Sixty engineers already use Claude Code with one shared Anthropic key. Finance sees a single invoice. Security has been asked whether card data has gone into a prompt.

  • See. The shared key moves into Gantry and laptops point at the gateway. Nothing is blocked for two weeks.
  • Know. Engineers run gantry login and get a personal key through the identity provider. The shared key is revoked.
  • Limit. A daily budget per person and a monthly budget per team.
  • Protect. The secrets guardrail runs in shadow, then blocks. Personal data is masked.
  • What they have: spend by engineer and team, a refusal message developers can act on, and a log that answers the card data question with records.
Example: a platform team with 40 MCP servers

Agents across the company call internal MCP servers for GitHub, the data warehouse and deploys. Each server has its own token, and nobody can list which agent may call which tool.

  • See. The servers go behind Gantry's one MCP endpoint. Every tool call is recorded with the person who made it.
  • Know. People sign in with their key or by OAuth from the client. Each rule lists the tools its team may see.
  • Limit. Merges and deploys wait for an admin's approval. Any tool has a kill switch.
  • Protect. Each tool is pinned by a hash of its description and schema. A changed tool is held until an admin approves it.
  • What they have: one list of every tool, who called it, and a way to stop one tool without taking a server down.
Example: a healthcare company that must keep patient data in region

Clinical teams want model help with notes. Compliance requires that patient data stays with an EU endpoint under a signed agreement, and that records can be produced on request.

  • See. Gantry runs in the company's own EU cloud account, from the Terraform module.
  • Limit. The clinical rule reaches only deployments labelled eu. A request with no EU deployment is refused before anything is sent.
  • Protect. Personal data is masked, and a regex guardrail masks patient record numbers.
  • Prove. With Gantry Enterprise: export to the SIEM, a retention period, and a legal hold when a case opens.
  • What they have: a record per request naming the region rule that decided it. Gantry enforces the label set on each deployment. It does not check where a provider runs.
