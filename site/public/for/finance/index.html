Use casesFor finance

"Who spent this, and will the budget last the month?"

The provider invoice gives one total per account. It does not say which team, tool or person spent it. Gantry counts every request as it passes, under the name of whoever sent it.

What you get

Spend with names
Per organization, team, person, key and model, from the first day tools point at the gateway.
Budgets that hold
A team that reaches its budget is refused until the period starts again or someone raises the number. Periods run from a minute to a year.
A forecast per budget
Each budget says whether it lasts the period at the current pace, or the date it runs out.
A trial before any cap
A budget can record what it would refuse for a month before it refuses anything.

The console's Spend page, on test data. The dashed line is the daily pace that keeps every monthly budget inside its limit.

What you decide

The engineer adds the lines. The numbers are yours.

How the numbers relate to the invoice

  • Gantry computes cost from the tokens each answer reports and a price per model. Prices for most models are built in, and your own rates can be set in the policy.
  • Expect the totals to be close to the provider invoice and not identical, for example where you have a negotiated discount.
  • Only requests that go through Gantry are counted. Subscription seats, such as Claude Team, are billed by the vendor per seat. Their requests go through Gantry too, and their cost shows as an estimate at API prices.
  • Alerts before a budget runs out, to Slack, Teams, PagerDuty or email, are part of Gantry Enterprise. Enterprise
